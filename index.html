⚙️ Recommendations Before We Launch
  • No Special Ad Category Restrictions: Unlike real estate listings or credit offers, roofing contractor lead generation does not fall under Meta's Special Ad Categories (Housing/Credit/Employment), allowing precision radius targeting across Chelan and Douglas counties without artificial demographic restrictions.
  • Speed-to-Lead Automation (Under 60 Seconds): Contractor lead conversion rates decline drastically if uncontacted within 15 minutes. Connect these Meta Instant Forms directly into GoHighLevel to trigger an instant SMS confirmation to the homeowner and push-notify Samuel or the dispatch phone with a 1-tap call button.
  • Seasonal Creative Rhythm: Heavy push for Concept 01 (Replacements) and Concept 02 (Inspections) in late summer through fall before snow season. Shift budget toward Concept 03 (Rapid Leak Repair) during early spring freeze-thaw cycles.
  • Verify Direct Dispatch Number: Ensure all call extensions and ad descriptions align with the official company phone number (509) 630-3445 shown on Apex's fleet trucks and website header.
